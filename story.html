Our family

A Buffalo restaurant family, since the Charlesgate

When you order from Giacobbi's, you're ordering from a family that has cooked for Western New York for generations: the Charlesgate, Giacobbi's on Transit, Carmine's, and now Allen Street.

Where it started

The Charlesgate

The family's landmark restaurant on Transit Road in Williamsville, remembered as a local legend. The Buffalo News reviewed it in November 1987.

The '90s

Giacobbi's Italian Oven & Rotisserie

Opened at 4715 Transit Road on the Charlesgate site. It served Sicilian specialties, wood-fired pizza and family-style portions meant to feed the whole table. A second location, Giacobbi's Trattoria, opened on Main Street at Eggert.

Decades on Transit

Carmine's

For years the family ran Carmine's on Transit Road. That's where the eggplant lasagna became a favorite. A fire closed it in 2015.

2016 to today

Giacobbi's Cucina Citta

Cousins Billy and Mike Jacobbi brought the family's recipes downtown to 59 Allen Street in Allentown and served the first meal in May 2016.

From the family binder

The kitchen still cooks from a binder of typed sheets in plastic sleeves, most of them carried over from Carmine's, with decades of pencil and marker on top. Here are a few, exactly as written. Quantities are restaurant batches, so you may want to cut them down.

Carmine's → Giacobbi's

Greens

Yield: 1 Fish Bin

The base of our Beans & Greens and the Steak Giacobbi. It started as a Carmine's sheet: someone crossed out "Carmines," wrote in "Giacobbi's," and swapped the frozen collards for Swiss chard or rapini.

Giacobbi's · Swiss chard or Rapini

Read the card

Ingredients

  • 1Case Escarole
  • 2 Bags Chopped Swiss chard or Rapini
  • 1/2Cup Seasoning Salt
  • 1.5Cups Olive Oil
  • 1Cup Minced Garlic
  • 1/2 Cup Chicken Consume
  • 1Tbls Black Pepper
  • 1Tbls Crushed Red Pepper

Procedure

Bring large stock pot 3/4's full of water to a boil. Add Escarole and Greens and cook till escarole is soft – approx 10-12minutes – Drain, shock with cold water. Press and drain in colander using a small pizza pan with a 5 gallon bucket of water on top for at least 1/2 an hour. Then add spices and oils.

Pasta Fazool

Yield: 4 Gallons

On today's menu as Pasta e Fagiole, marked "family recipe." The kitchen makes it four gallons at a time.

Read the card

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 gal Water
  • 1/2 cont Ham base
  • 2 Teas. Black Pepper
  • 1/2 Teas. Crushed Red Pepper
  • 2 cup diced Carrots
  • 2 cup diced Onions
  • 2 cup diced Celery
  • 1 ricotta bkt. Marinara sauce
  • 3 #10 cans Great Northern Beans
  • 1 lb Bacon … Diced and well browned
  • 1 teasp Liquid Smoke
  • 1 cup Sherry wine
  • 1 large ladle Marinara sc
  • 2 cups Parm cheese

Procedure

Use the the large 8 gal pot. Add water, base, spices & cut veg to stock pot. Bring to boil. Then add the can of Marinara & boil again. Next add 2 & 1/2 cans of Great Northern beans. Still well – & often!

* While you are boiling the stock & veg take the bacon and brown in a large sauté pan. Brown to crisp and add sherry, smoke and marinara. Puree this mix with other 1/2 can of Great Northern Beans.

When soup is almost done add this bean paste mix to thicken the soup. Still well. Add parmesan and stir. Turn down the burner & simmer for 40 min.

When done – stir well and divide into 5 or 6 buckets and chill.

Meat Balls (10 Pound Batch)

Yield: 1 Fish Bin

The ten-pound batch. For a busy weekend, the card says to do several twenty-pound mixes.

Read the card

Ingredients

  • 1 lg onion-minced
  • 1 tbl minced garlic
  • 5 Lbs. Ground Beef
  • 5 Lbs. Bulk Sausage
  • 2 Cups Italian Bread Crumbs
  • 1 Cup Parm Cheese
  • 1 teas. Pepper
  • 1/2 Cup Dry Oregano/Basil Mix
  • 1 tbl Fresh basil
  • 3 Eggs

Procedure

Pull meat ahead of time and put into bus pan and let come close to room temp.(Makes Mixing Easier). Sautee garlic and onion until clear. Mix all ingredients and meat together until COMPLETELY combined. Roll meatballs into 5 oz smooth balls. Put 4x7 in hotel pans, foil covered, with an inch of water in pan in regular ovens at 400 degrees for 45 minutes. Drain and allow to cool. For larger batches do multiple 20 pound mixes.
