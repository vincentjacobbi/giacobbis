Our family

A Buffalo restaurant family, since the Charlesgate

When you order from Giacobbi's, you're ordering from a family that has cooked for Western New York for generations: the Charlesgate, Giacobbi's on Transit, Carmine's, and now Allen Street.

Where it started

The Charlesgate

The family's first restaurant, on Transit Road in Williamsville, became a local legend. The Buffalo News reviewed it in November 1987.

The '90s

Giacobbi's Italian Oven & Rotisserie

Opened at 4715 Transit Road on the Charlesgate site. It served Sicilian specialties, wood-fired pizza and family-style portions meant to feed the whole table. A second location, Giacobbi's Trattoria, opened on Main Street at Eggert.

Decades on Transit

Carmine's

For years the family ran Carmine's on Transit Road. That's where the eggplant lasagna became a favorite. A fire closed it in 2015.

2016 to today

Giacobbi's Cucina Citta

Cousins Billy and Mike Jacobbi brought the family's recipes downtown to 59 Allen Street in Allentown and served the first meal in May 2016.
